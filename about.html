About Overkill Detailing

Hi, I’m Josh — the founder of Overkill Detailing. My passion for cars started when I was just 12 years old. Whether fixing them, cleaning them, or perfecting every curve and corner, I was drawn to the craft. Now at 17, after years of experience and growth, I’ve transformed that passion into a premium detailing service that delivers showroom-quality results at an honest price.

Starting in Weston, Florida, Overkill Detailing has expanded to serve the entire Broward and Miami area — from Boca down to South Beach. Every detail is handled with care, precision, and dedication to quality. When you book with us, you’re not just getting a wash; you’re getting a commitment to excellence.
