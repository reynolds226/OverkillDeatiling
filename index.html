About OverkillDetailing

My name is Josh, and I’ve had a lifelong passion for cars. I started detailing when I was just 12 years old, and what began as a weekend hobby quickly turned into a business built on precision and pride. Now at 17, after five years of hands-on experience, I’ve learned how to deliver outstanding results at cost-effective rates — keeping services affordable while ensuring every vehicle I touch looks its absolute best. OverkillDetailing was born and raised in Weston, Florida, and now serves the entire Broward and Miami area. Once I got my license, I made the business fully mobile — bringing professional detailing directly to you for maximum convenience and top-quality service.

Our Packages & Pricing

Deep vacuum, full interior wipe-down, interior protectant (3-month duration), refreshed carpets and mats cleaned and drill-brushed with fabric cleaner and softener for a new look and feel.

Full Interior Detail

Sedans: $145 | SUVs/Pickups: $155 | Minivans/Vans: $165

Includes deep vacuuming, carpet and seat steam-clean, stain removal, cloth/leather seat treatment, and leather conditioner. Exterior Basic Clean Up

Sedans: $60 | SUVs/Pickups: $75 | Minivans/Vans: $75

Sedans: $60 | SUVs/Pickups/Minivans/Vans: $75
